Personalized Sofa ("we", "our", "us") provides a platform for users to design, visualize, and refine personalized furniture experiences through 3D AI-based model generation and concierge guidance. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal data when you use the Personalized Sofa mobile application (“App”).

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that helps personalize your experience and improve our AI-driven design and concierge services.

A. User Account Information

  • Name, email address, and password (secured via Firebase Authentication).
  • Profile preferences such as preferred style, usage type, material, and color choices.

B. Personalization & Design Data

  • Your input selections for sofa personalization (usage style, seating feel, color, fabric, material, pattern, stitching, and leg style).
  • 3D model generation requests and selected refinements.

C. Concierge & Chatbot Interactions

  • Messages or chat interactions between you and the in-app bot or concierge team.
  • Session data to support context-aware responses (not shared with third parties).

D. Device Data

  • Device type, OS version, app version, and general performance logs (for improving service reliability).

2. How We Use Information

  • Personalization: to generate and visualize customized sofa models based on your preferences.
  • 3D Rendering: to send your design data to the AI engine (Meshy API) for creating photorealistic models.
  • Concierge Support: to connect you with design experts or in-app AI assistants for tailored advice.
  • Communication: to share design updates, feature improvements, and concierge appointment details.
  • Account Management: to authenticate users and maintain account information securely.

3. AI & 3D Processing (Meshy)

We use third-party AI infrastructure (e.g., Meshy API) to generate 3D visualizations of your personalized sofa. Only the information required to render your chosen configuration—such as color, material, seating capacity, and design traits—is transmitted. Data is not permanently stored by the AI provider.

Note: All transmissions are encrypted and processed securely according to Meshy’s privacy and data protection standards.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell or rent your data. We may share limited data only as needed to operate the App:

  • Service Providers: For authentication, hosting, AI rendering, and concierge operations (e.g., Firebase, Meshy).
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law or to protect rights, property, or safety.

5. Security

  • All communications are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • User credentials are securely managed through Firebase Authentication.
  • Access to concierge or admin features is restricted and monitored.

6. Permissions & Consent

The App may request access to limited features for functionality:

  • Camera: Optional, to capture reference photos for better design matching.
  • Notifications: To inform you about design completion, concierge updates, or saved preferences.

7. Data Retention & Deletion

We retain your data only as long as needed to provide our services. You can delete your account and all associated data anytime.

Delete Account: Visit Delete Your Account. All personal data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

8. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us for immediate deletion.

9. Your Rights

  • Access: View or update your personal data within the App.
  • Deletion: Request account and data deletion anytime.
  • Portability: Request a copy of your data via email.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect improvements or regulatory requirements. Changes will be communicated in-app or via email.
