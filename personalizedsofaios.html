Privacy Policy – meSofa

Last Updated: November 12, 2025

Welcome to meSofa. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use the meSofa mobile application (“App”). The app allows users to design personalized sofas using 3D visualization technology and receive recommendations from concierge services. This policy is designed to comply with Apple App Store guidelines and international privacy standards including GDPR and CCPA.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and password used for creating and managing your account.
  • Personalization Data: Sofa preferences such as seating type, size, fabric, color, materials, patterns, and other customization selections.
  • Concierge Communication: Messages and interactions with in-app concierge support or chat assistants.
  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, app version, and diagnostic information used to improve app stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To generate personalized 3D sofa models based on your customization preferences.
  • To provide concierge recommendations and customer support.
  • To notify you about design updates, saved configurations, and concierge appointments.
  • To improve app performance and user experience.

3. AI & 3D Processing

meSofa may use AI-powered rendering services to generate 3D visualizations of personalized sofas. Only the information required to generate the design (such as material, color, style, and dimensions) is transmitted to the rendering engine. This information is processed securely and is not permanently stored by the AI service provider.

4. Data Linked to You

The following types of data may be linked to your identity in accordance with Apple’s App Store privacy labels:

  • Contact Information (name and email address)
  • User Content (sofa customization preferences)
  • Identifiers (user account ID)
  • Diagnostics (crash logs and performance data)

5. Data Not Collected

We do not collect financial payment data within the app. We also do not track your activity across other apps or websites for advertising purposes.

6. Permissions

  • Camera (Optional): May be used to capture reference images to help design matching or room visualization.
  • Notifications: Used to notify users when designs are ready or when concierge support responds.

7. Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We may share limited information only with trusted service providers that support app functionality, such as authentication services, cloud hosting providers, or AI rendering services.

8. Data Storage and Security

  • All data is transmitted securely using HTTPS encryption.
  • User authentication and secure account management are handled through trusted authentication providers.
  • Access to user data is restricted and monitored.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to provide the app’s services. Users can request deletion of their account and associated data at any time.

Delete Account: Delete Your Account

All associated data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days.

10. Children’s Privacy

The meSofa app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has submitted personal data, we will delete the information promptly.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect improvements to the app or regulatory changes. If significant updates occur, users will be notified through the app or via email.
