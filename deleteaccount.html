Delete Your Personalized Sofa Data

Developer/App name: Personalized Sofa

You can request either a full account deletion or selective data deletion. Please follow the instructions below.

Option 1 — Delete Your Account (All Data)

Use this option to permanently delete your account and all associated data from Personalized Sofa.

  1. Email us from the address linked to your Personalized Sofa account.
  2. Use subject line: “Delete My Personalized Sofa Account”.
  3. If emailing from a different address, include your account email in the message body.

Request Account Deletion

Data deleted in this process

  • User account information (name, email, password hash)
  • Personalization preferences (sofa style, materials, color, etc.)
  • AI model generation history
  • Concierge chat history
  • Session tokens and authentication records

Retention: All data is permanently deleted within 7 business days, including backups.

Option 2 — Delete Specific Data (Keep Account)

Use this option if you wish to keep your account active but delete certain data categories.

  1. Email us from your registered account email.
  2. Use subject line: “Personalized Sofa Data Deletion Request (Keep Account)”.
  3. In the message, list which data you want deleted (see examples below).

Request Data Deletion

Data you may request deleted

  • AI model generation history
  • Chatbot or concierge messages
  • Saved designs, 3D render references, and preferences
  • Device session or configuration data

Data retained if you keep your account

  • Login credentials (email + hashed password)
  • Basic account identifiers needed for authentication

Retention: Selected data will be permanently deleted within 7 business days. Legal logs may be retained only as required by regulation.
